OIL INDIA LIMITED - DULIAJAN FIELD
NIGHT SHIFT LOG - 2026-01-26 - OCS-4 AND ADJOINING INSTALLATIONS

2245 hrs. During the changeover of the crude transfer pump the operator found
the discharge valve car-sealed open with no tag and no entry in the valve
register. The line was under pressure at 12 bar and a bleed was attempted before
the isolation was verified. Work was stopped and the area barricaded.

2320 hrs. A contractor welder was found working on the pipe rack platform with
his harness lanyard hooked to the scaffold coupler. The scaffold tag had expired
on 20 January and the platform had no toe board on the open side.

0110 hrs. Gas detector at the manifold area alarmed at 12 ppm H2S. Two operators
in the vicinity withdrew upwind and donned escape sets. The area was found to be
a flange weep on the test separator inlet; the permit for the adjacent hot work
was suspended.

0345 hrs. Diesel spillage of about ten litres near the generator shed while
topping up. Contained with sand, collected in a drum and handed to the waste
yard. No drain was affected and no injury resulted.

0500 hrs. The approach road to the well pad was found blocked by an unlit parked
tanker on a blind curve. The driver was resting in the cab without hazard lights
or cones. Journey management was informed and the vehicle moved at 0530 hrs.
