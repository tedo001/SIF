# SENTRA in thirty words

> Workers report near-misses every day. SENTRA reads each report the moment it is
> written, finds the ones that could have killed someone, shows why, and asks a
> person to confirm.

*Sense the Risk · Stop the Incident*

## If you have a minute instead of ten seconds

Oil India's crews file unsafe-act, unsafe-condition and near-miss reports every
shift. Most describe something minor. A few describe the exact conditions that
kill people - high energy, and a barrier that failed. Today those few are found
by hand, months later, if at all.

SENTRA reads every report as it arrives and scores it on one idea:
**fatal potential = high energy x failed barrier**. It maps each report to an
IOGP Life-Saving Rule, names the energy and the control that failed, and shows
the words it used to decide. Nothing is closed automatically: disagreements,
critical risk and thin evidence go to an HSE expert, and every decision that
expert makes becomes a label the model learns from.

It runs on a laptop, offline if the plant needs it, and reads Hindi, Marathi,
Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Urdu as well as English.
